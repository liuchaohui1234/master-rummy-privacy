This page is for users of Master Rummy, published by mohamed bady on Google Play (package name com.ahrnb.koo.flnmr). Use the steps below to request deletion of your account and associated data.

How to delete your account

Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Master Rummy and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning, type DELETE, and confirm.

Option 2 — Email

  1. Send an email to mohabad274@gmail.com with the subject Delete Account – Master Rummy.
  2. Include your in-game user ID or nickname, and the phone number or Facebook account used to log in (if any).
  3. We will verify the request and process it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Account identifiers (guest ID, phone login, Facebook login binding)
  • Profile data such as nickname and avatar
  • Friends list and in-game social records
  • Gameplay progress, virtual chips / gold, and task data
  • In-app chat history stored on our game servers

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Purchase / transaction records required for accounting, anti-fraud, or legal obligations may be kept for up to 24 months, or longer if the law requires.
  • Aggregated or de-identified analytics that cannot reasonably identify you may be retained.
  • Data already sent to independent third parties (for example Google Play, ad networks, Firebase) is handled under their own policies; we will stop using it for your account after deletion.

After deletion

You will not be able to recover the same account, chips, or progress. You may create a new account later, which starts as a new user.
